Original Radiance HDR: 10000 × 5000, retained in models/reference/space-spheremaps/.
Browser assets are generated by scripts/import-space-hdri.py from that original:
- multi-nebulae-2-rgbm.png: 4096 × 2048, RGBM16, exposure 12, native WebGL panorama.
- multi-nebulae-2.webp: tone-mapped version for Three.js and CSS fallback.
- multi-nebulae-2.json: provenance, original SHA-256 and conversion settings.

Sky changes affect rendering only. Galaxy, system and colony share the same world-space orientation. Battle and practice use their actual camera orientation without a translating sky sphere. Scene exposure is lower in fleet overview to keep weapons readable. No external texture requests are needed at runtime.
